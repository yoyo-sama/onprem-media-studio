Long take

The brief is its screenplay, or import one. Two kinds of images, both optional: the first frame, where the video starts, and references, who or what must stay the same. With neither, the first segment is generated from the text. Nothing renders yet: the long take opens on its segments, where each one can also take an end image.

Target markets

One job per selected market, with the prompt adapted to it. None selected: the prompt as it is.

Extra references

Who or what the video keeps: photos or sheets of the characters, a setting, objects; clips (a camera movement, or a subject) and sounds (a voice, music, ambience). Each one gets its role. No image yet? Make a sheet from the brief below. Nothing to review: Generate renders the video at once.

Who or what must stay the same from one segment to the next: a photo or a sheet of a character, an object, a setting. Each one gets its role; they are never shown as they are. With no first frame, the first segment is made from them.

Qwen Image 2.1 takes up to 10 images in all. The input image is <image1>; your references are <image2>, <image3>… in the order of the list below. Cite them in your instruction, for example: “put the character of <image1> in the setting of <image2>”. The result keeps the input image's size; each reference makes the render longer.

Each image gets a subject number, each clip and sound its own label, used automatically in the prompt. A clip is sent silent: to use its sound, add the sound itself.
